Event information

About the hackathon

Sankalp’2026 is AJIET’s 3rd edition of its national-level 24-hour hackathon, organised by the IEEE Student Branch in association with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering. Teams will develop solutions to real-world challenges and present their work at A.J. Institute of Engineering and Technology, Mangaluru.

Who can participate

Students pursuing BE/BTech, MCA, and Diploma programs are welcome to participate.

Team rules

Each team may include up to 3 members. Provide complete name, USN, and email details for every participating member.

Abstract requirements

Choose one of the six problem statements and submit a one-page abstract explaining the problem, proposed solution, approach, and potential impact. Accepted file formats are PDF, DOC, or DOCX, up to 3 MB.

Selection and event

Industry and academic mentors will evaluate submissions. Acceptance notifications are scheduled for 15 November 2026. Shortlisted teams complete final registration from 16 to 25 November, then take part in the 24-hour hackathon on 27–28 November.

Registration fee and prizes

The final registration fee is ₹600 per shortlisted team. Teams will compete for a total prize pool of ₹75,000.

Abstract deadline and venue

Submit your abstract by 10 November 2026. The event takes place at A.J. Institute of Engineering and Technology, NH-66, Kottara Chowki, Mangaluru.

Problem statements

Six real-world challenges. Room to build boldly.

01 · AI-Enabled Drone & Counter-Drone Threat Simulation Trainer

Explore AI-driven simulation and training systems designed to study drone-related threats and improve threat-response preparedness.

02 · AI-Powered Smart Food Waste Reduction & Sustainable Redistribution Ecosystem for Institutional Kitchens and Food Processing Units

Develop intelligent solutions for institutional kitchens and food processing units to reduce food waste and support sustainable redistribution.

03 · V2V Visionaries: Engineering the Next Generation of Collision Avoidance & Road Safety

Reimagine safer roads through vehicle-to-vehicle communication, intelligent sensing, and next-generation collision avoidance solutions.

04 · Edge-AI Smart Stethoscope for Real-Time Cardiopulmonary Screening in Rural PHCs

Explore smart, edge-enabled stethoscope systems that support preliminary cardiopulmonary screening in rural Primary Health Centres.

05 · On-Site Containment-Based Toilets: Innovative Solutions for Sustainable Waste Treatment

Explore technological, biochemical, or nature-based approaches for treating excreta on-site while protecting the environment and groundwater.

06 · Indigenous Leak Detection Sensor Systems for Water Supply Pipelines

Develop indigenous sensor-based systems for detecting pipeline leaks and improving water-supply operation and maintenance.
